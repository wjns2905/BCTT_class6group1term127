## Vision & Editorial Stance
HEART.ICLES is an authoritative, compassionate multimedia digital newspaper designed to counter sensationalism with human-centered, rigorously sourced storytelling. Rooted in the linguistic and communicative excellence of ULIS - VNU, the publication bridges domestic community narratives with international investigative standards.

## Target Audience Personas
1. **Linh (20, ULIS Language Student):** Seeks polished bilingual stories, culture, education policies, and aesthetic reading experiences without clickbait.
2. **Mr. Quang (48, Academic & Parent):** Values in-depth reporting on national assembly debates, higher education funding, and heritage preservation.
3. **Thao (26, NGO Communications Specialist):** Reads human interest stories, community empowerment initiatives, and mutual aid reporting.
4. **David (32, International Researcher in Hanoi):** Follows Vietnamese societal trends, urban development, and translated reports with transparent sourcing.
5. **Grandma Mai (68, Retired Educator):** Uses text-to-speech audio reader and large font mode to read stories about older generations and healthcare.

## Core Information Architecture
1. **Trending:** Breaking national socio-economic policy, inspirational stories, viral societal movements.
2. **Society:** Education regulations, community mutual aid, climate resilience, public transport.
3. **Human Stories:** Deep portraits of resilience, extraordinary compassion, volunteers, and survival.
4. **Mind & Soul:** Mental wellbeing, art, nutrition science, contemplative living.
5. **In-depth:** Longform investigative journalism, Hanoi cultural memory, craftsmanship revivals.
6. **About Us:** Editorial manifesto, board structure, ethical guidelines, contact points.

## Key Interaction Affordances
- 3-column CNN/Guardian hero bento grid.
- Interactive reading suite (A-/A+ font scaler, Web Speech API text-to-speech, read progress bar, verified source attribution).
- Live search modal with instant multi-field query matching.
- Community feedback & comment persistence via `localStorage`.
- Reader poll with dynamic live percentage visualization.
- Multimedia video hub with 5 embedded broadcast reports.
- Audio radio podcast player.
